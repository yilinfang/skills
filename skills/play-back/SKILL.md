name: play-back
description: Restate my goals and the problem I'm trying to solve, in your own words.
disable-model-invocation: true
---

Play back what you think I'm after. In your own words, restate:

- **Goals**: the outcome I want, and why I want it.
- **Problem**: what stands between me and that outcome.

Mark which parts I said and which parts you inferred. Then stop and ask me to confirm or correct you. Change nothing until I do.
